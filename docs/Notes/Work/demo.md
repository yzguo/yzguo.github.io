## Context

A three-person reading group wants to keep its notes in one place. In this sample, the goal is to turn a folder of loose text files into a small Markdown site with a table of contents, and my role is to set up the file structure and check the final pages.

## Progress

- Week 1 — Listed the existing files and agreed on one folder per topic.
- Week 2 — Added a shared page template and converted the first three notes.
- Week 3 — Went through the index links with the other two members and fixed six broken anchors.

## Outcome

The sample site builds with 12 pages and has no broken internal links. The remaining risk is that old links from chat threads may point to files that have been renamed.
