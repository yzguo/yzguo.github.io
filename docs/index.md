## Welcome

I’m Yanzhen, an undergraduate physics student at the [University of Science and Technology of China](https://www.ustc.edu.cn/). I like understanding how things work—and then finding a way to build, model, or explain them.

This site is a living notebook: a place for work in progress, finished projects, and ideas worth keeping. It’s built from Markdown, so its content can grow without needing a web-development workflow.
